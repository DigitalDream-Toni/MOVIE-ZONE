What is this?

Every movie, series, and episode can store a download URL and a watch URL. This page pings each one (one request per host, with a short timeout) and reports the ones that are unreachable. A row here means visitors clicking Download or Watch on the public site will hit a broken or erroring link. Fix by updating the URL for that title to a working address.
